- This Page is Just Getting Started -

Concrete ways to get involved: canvassing, phone banking, local event coordination, and more, are in the works, but what we do have so far is below.  Check back soon, or reach out below and we’ll find a place for you sooner.  Even better, if you have a question or a comment, please email us by clicking the CONTACT THE CAMPAIGN button, below.

Positions Needed (So Far!):

  • State / County / Precinct Organizers:
    Build and lead the campaign’s ground-level presence in your state, county, or precinct.  You’d be the person who knows the local landscape, recruits neighbors, and makes sure we’re on the ballot and in the conversation where it counts.
  • Communications Personnel:
    Help shape and carry the campaign’s message across platforms and media.  This includes drafting press materials, managing social media accounts, coordinating with local media, and ensuring our voice stays clear and consistent.
  • IT / Website Personnel:
    Maintain and improve the campaign’s digital infrastructure.  If you can write HTML, wrangle a database, manage a CMS, or keep servers running, we need your skills to keep this operation functional and secure.
  • State and Local Issues Scouts:
    Research and report on issues that matter in your state and community but aren’t making national headlines.  Your job is to make sure the campaign understands what’s actually happening on the ground, not just what’s on cable news.
  • Community Pulse Takers:
    Gauge public sentiment on local and state-level issues through informal and structured outreach.  We need people who can listen systematically, at town halls, community meetings, and front doors, and bring back what they hear in a form the campaign can act on.
  • Writers:
    Draft policy briefs, blog posts, op-eds, talking points, and other written materials that carry the campaign’s voice.  If you can write clearly about complex issues and meet a deadline, we have work for you.
  • Press Contacts:
    Serve as designated points of contact for journalists and media outlets.  You’d field inquiries, coordinate interviews, and make sure the press can always reach a real person who can speak on behalf of the campaign.
  • Military and Government Liaison Personnel:
    Help the campaign engage productively with active-duty military, veterans, civil servants, and government offices at every level.  Experience navigating these institutions, their language, protocols, and concerns, is invaluable.
  • Drivers:
    Provide reliable transportation for campaign staff, the candidate, and materials to and from events.  A clean driving record and familiarity with your region are the main requirements.
  • A/V Roadies:
    Set up, operate, and tear down audio and video equipment for campaign events.  If you know your way around a PA system, a projector, or a livestream rig, we need you on site.
  • Event Schedulers:
    Coordinate the logistics of campaign events: booking venues, confirming dates, managing invitations, and making sure nothing falls through the cracks.  This role requires organizational discipline and a talent for keeping multiple moving parts on track.
  • Event Support Personnel:
    Be the hands on the ground at campaign events: greeting attendees, managing sign-in, distributing materials, handling setup and cleanup, and making sure every event runs smoothly from first arrival to last handshake.
